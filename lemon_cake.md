#Lemon cake recipe
## Ingredients
* Lemon
* Sugar
* Flour
* Baking powder
* Eggs
* Milk
* Butter

##
1. Melt Butter and add sugar sugar and cream until fluffy
2. Fold in flour and baking powder
3. Make a well
4. Crack one egg at a time and whisk
5. Add milk and stir till just combined
6. Cook at 180 degrees for 45 minutes
7. While baking, add lemon juice and sugar to a pot
8. Heat and stir on low heat until sugar is dissolved
9. Pour syrup on still hot cake and leave to cool
10. Enjoy!
